# ============ 1 Introduction ============
CH@@1@@1. Introduction@@1. 引言
LEAD@@引言提出全篇要解决的问题：深度很重要，但"堆更多层"行不通。作者用图 1 证明 56 层 plain 网络的训练误差<strong>比 20 层还高</strong>——这不是过拟合（训练误差也高），而是"退化问题"。这段是理解整篇论文的起点。
P@@Deep convolutional neural networks have led to a series of breakthroughs for image classification. Deep networks naturally integrate low/mid/high-level features and classifiers in an end-to-end multi-layer fashion, and the "levels" of features can be enriched by the number of stacked layers (depth).@@深度卷积神经网络带来了一系列图像分类的突破。深度网络以端到端的多层方式，自然地整合低/中/高层特征与分类器，而特征的"层次"可以通过堆叠层数（深度）来丰富。
P@@Recent evidence reveals that network depth is of crucial importance, and the leading results on the challenging ImageNet dataset all exploit "very deep" models, with a depth of sixteen to thirty. Many other non-trivial visual recognition tasks have also greatly benefited from very deep models.@@近期证据表明，网络深度至关重要；在极具挑战性的 ImageNet 数据集上，领先的结果都采用"非常深"的模型，深度从 16 层到 30 层。许多其它不平凡的视觉识别任务也极大地受益于非常深的模型。
P@@Driven by the significance of depth, a question arises: Is learning better networks as easy as stacking more layers? An obstacle to answering this question was the notorious problem of vanishing/exploding gradients, which hamper convergence from the beginning. This problem, however, has been largely addressed by normalized initialization and intermediate normalization layers, which enable networks with tens of layers to start converging for SGD with backpropagation.@@在深度重要性的驱动下，一个问题出现了：学习更好的网络是否就像堆叠更多层一样容易？回答这个问题的一个障碍是臭名昭著的<strong>梯度消失/爆炸</strong>问题，它从一开始就阻碍收敛。不过，这个问题已在很大程度上被归一化初始化和中间归一化层（BatchNorm）解决，使几十层的网络能够用反向传播 SGD 开始收敛。
P@@When deeper networks are able to start converging, a degradation problem has been exposed: with the network depth increasing, accuracy gets saturated (which might be unsurprising) and then degrades rapidly. Unexpectedly, such degradation is not caused by overfitting, and adding more layers to a suitably deep model leads to higher training error.@@当更深的网络能够开始收敛时，一个<strong>退化问题</strong>暴露出来：随着深度增加，准确率趋于饱和（这并不奇怪），然后迅速下降。出乎意料的是，这种退化并非由过拟合引起——向一个已经足够深的模型添加更多层，反而导致更高的<strong>训练误差</strong>。
NOTE@@这句是全文的"问题陈述"：过拟合是训练误差低、测试误差高；退化是<strong>训练误差本身就高</strong>。分清二者是理解 ResNet 的前提。作者后面强调：用了 BatchNorm 后梯度消失已被排除，退化另有原因。
FIG@@fig1_degradation.png@@Figure 1. Training error (left) and test error (right) on CIFAR-10 with 20-layer and 56-layer plain networks. The deeper network has higher training error, and thus test error.@@图 1 · 20 层与 56 层「plain」网络在 CIFAR-10 上的训练误差（左）与测试误差（右）。更深的网络训练误差更高，测试误差也更高。@@这张图是整个 ResNet 的"问题现场"：56 层（红）从头到尾训练误差都比 20 层（黄）高。如果只是过拟合，训练误差应该更低才对。这个反直觉现象逼出了残差学习。
P@@The degradation indicates that not all systems are similarly easy to optimize. Let us consider a shallower architecture and its deeper counterpart that adds more layers onto it. There exists a solution by construction to the deeper model: the added layers are identity mapping, and the other layers are copied from the learned shallower model. The existence of this constructed solution indicates that a deeper model should produce no higher training error than its shallower counterpart. But experiments show that our current solvers on hand are unable to find solutions that are comparably good or better than the constructed solution.@@退化表明，并非所有系统都同样容易优化。考虑一个较浅的架构及其通过添加更多层得到的更深对应模型。对更深的模型，存在一个<strong>构造性解</strong>：新增层做恒等映射，其它层从已学好的浅层模型复制过来。这个构造性解的存在表明，更深模型的训练误差不应高于其浅层对应模型。但实验显示，我们现有的求解器无法找到与这个构造性解相当或更好的解。
NOTE@@<b>全篇最重要的论证</b>：这是一个"存在性 vs 可学习性"的矛盾——理论上更深的模型至少不比浅层差（把多出的层设为恒等映射即可），但优化器找不到这个解。所以问题不是模型容量，而是<b>优化难度</b>。ResNet 的核心就是把"学恒等映射"这个解变得容易到达。
P@@In this paper, we address the degradation problem by introducing a deep residual learning framework. Instead of hoping each few stacked layers directly fit a desired underlying mapping, we explicitly let these layers fit a residual mapping. Formally, denoting the desired underlying mapping as H(x), we let the stacked nonlinear layers fit another mapping of F(x) := H(x) − x. The original mapping is recast into F(x) + x. We hypothesize that it is easier to optimize the residual mapping than to optimize the original, unreferenced mapping. To the extreme, if an identity mapping were optimal, it would be easier to push the residual to zero than to fit an identity mapping by a stack of nonlinear layers.@@本文通过引入深度残差学习框架来解决退化问题。我们不再期望每几层堆叠层直接拟合一个目标映射，而是明确让这些层拟合一个<strong>残差映射</strong>。形式化地，把目标映射记为 H(x)，我们让堆叠的非线性层拟合另一个映射 F(x) := H(x) − x。原映射被改写为 F(x) + x。我们假设：优化残差映射比优化原始的无参照映射更容易。极端情况下，若恒等映射是最优的，那么把残差推向零，比用一堆非线性层去拟合恒等映射更容易。
NOTE@@<b>一句话讲透残差</b>：你想让几层学会"什么都不做"（恒等映射 H(x)=x），用普通非线性层很难（要精确学到权重组合恰好抵消）；但如果改成学 F(x)=H(x)−x，只要把权重推向 0，F(x)→0，输出就是 x。<b>把"学会恒等"变成"把残差归零"</b>——后者对优化器友好得多。
P@@The formulation of F(x) + x can be realized by feedforward neural networks with "shortcut connections". Shortcut connections are those skipping one or more layers. In our case, the shortcut connections simply perform identity mapping, and their outputs are added to the outputs of the stacked layers. Identity shortcut connections add neither extra parameter nor computational complexity. The entire network can still be trained end-to-end by SGD with backpropagation, and can be easily implemented using common libraries without modifying the solvers.@@F(x) + x 的形式可以通过带"短路连接"的前馈网络实现。短路连接是跳过一层或多层的连接。在我们的情形中，短路连接只做恒等映射，其输出与堆叠层的输出相加。恒等短路连接既不增加参数，也不增加计算复杂度。整个网络仍可用 SGD 加反向传播端到端训练，并且无需修改求解器即可用常见库轻松实现。
P@@We show that: 1) Our extremely deep residual nets are easy to optimize, but the counterpart "plain" nets (that simply stack layers) exhibit higher training error when the depth increases; 2) Our deep residual nets can easily enjoy accuracy gains from greatly increased depth, producing results substantially better than previous networks.@@我们表明：1）我们极深的残差网络易于优化，而对应的"plain"网络（简单堆叠层）随深度增加表现出更高的训练误差；2）我们的深度残差网络能轻松从大幅增加的深度中获得精度提升，结果显著优于此前网络。
P@@On the ImageNet classification dataset, we obtain excellent results by extremely deep residual nets. Our 152-layer residual net is the deepest network ever presented on ImageNet, while still having lower complexity than VGG nets. Our ensemble has 3.57% top-5 error on the ImageNet test set, and won the 1st place in the ILSVRC 2015 classification competition.@@在 ImageNet 分类数据集上，我们用极深残差网络取得优异结果。152 层残差网络是当时 ImageNet 上呈现过的最深网络，而复杂度仍低于 VGG 网络。我们的集成在 ImageNet 测试集上取得 3.57% 的 top-5 错误率，赢得 ILSVRC 2015 分类竞赛第一名。

CH@@2@@2. Related Work@@2. 相关工作
LEAD@@这节把残差思想的历史脉络梳理了一遍：从 VLAD/Fisher Vector 的残差编码，到 Multigrid 的多尺度残差求解，再到 highway networks 的门控短路。重点是最后一段对 highway networks 的对比——它澄清了 ResNet 的"恒等短路"与前人"门控短路"的本质区别。
P@@Residual Representations: In image recognition, VLAD is a representation that encodes by the residual vectors with respect to a dictionary, and Fisher Vector can be formulated as a probabilistic version of VLAD. Both of them are powerful shallow representations for image retrieval and classification. For vector quantization, encoding residual vectors is shown to be more effective than encoding original vectors.@@残差表示：在图像识别中，VLAD 是一种用相对于字典的残差向量进行编码的表示，Fisher 向量可表述为 VLAD 的概率版本。二者都是图像检索与分类中强大的浅层表示。在矢量量化中，编码残差向量被证明比编码原始向量更有效。
P@@In low-level vision and computer graphics, for solving Partial Differential Equations, the widely used Multigrid method reformulates the system as subproblems at multiple scales, where each subproblem is responsible for the residual solution between a coarser and a finer scale. An alternative to Multigrid is hierarchical basis preconditioning, which relies on variables that represent residual vectors between two scales. These methods suggest that a good reformulation or preconditioning can simplify the optimization.@@在底层视觉与计算机图形学中，为求解偏微分方程，广泛使用的多重网格方法把系统重新表述为多个尺度的子问题，每个子问题负责较粗与较细尺度之间的残差解。多重网格的替代方案是分层基预条件，它依赖表示两尺度间残差向量的变量。这些方法表明，良好的重新表述或预条件可以简化优化。
NOTE@@作者引用 Multigrid 是有深意的：一个老牌数值方法早就知道"解残差比解原问题容易"。ResNet 把这个思想搬进了深度学习——这不是凭空发明，而是把数值分析的老智慧移植过来。
P@@Shortcut Connections: Practices and theories that lead to shortcut connections have been studied for a long time. An early practice of training MLPs is to add a linear layer connected from the network input to the output. In some works, a few intermediate layers are directly connected to auxiliary classifiers for addressing vanishing/exploding gradients.@@短路连接：通向短路连接的实践与理论已被研究很久。训练多层感知机的一个早期实践，是添加一条从网络输入直接连到输出的线性层。在一些工作中，若干中间层直接连接到辅助分类器，以解决梯度消失/爆炸问题。
P@@Concurrent with our work, highway networks present shortcut connections with gating functions. These gates are data-dependent and have parameters, in contrast to our identity shortcuts that are parameter-free. When a gated shortcut is "closed" (approaching zero), the layers in highway networks represent non-residual functions. On the contrary, our formulation always learns residual functions; our identity shortcuts are never closed, and all information is always passed through, with additional residual functions to be learned. In addition, highway networks have not demonstrated accuracy gains with extremely increased depth.@@与我们同期的工作"highway networks"提出了带门控函数的短路连接。这些门依赖数据且带参数，与我们的无参数恒等短路形成对比。当门控短路"关闭"（趋近 0）时，highway 网络中的层表示非残差函数。相反，我们的表述总是学习残差函数；我们的恒等短路从不关闭，所有信息始终通过，同时学习额外的残差函数。此外，highway networks 并未展示在极深情况下的精度提升。
NOTE@@<b>ResNet 与 Highway 的关键区别</b>：Highway 的门是"可学习的、可能关闭的"，所以网络可能退回普通映射；ResNet 的短路是<b>永远打开的恒等映射</b>，保证残差学习始终发生。这个"不关闭"的设计，是 ResNet 能扩展到上百层的关键。

# ============ 3 Deep Residual Learning ============
CH@@3@@3. Deep Residual Learning@@3. 深度残差学习
LEAD@@全文技术核心。3.1 解释为什么残差形式更容易优化；3.2 给出公式与两种捷径（恒等 / 投影）；3.3 描述 ImageNet 上的 plain 与 residual 对照架构；3.4 是训练配置。读懂 3.1 和 3.2 就抓住了 ResNet 的全部思想。
SUB@@3.1 Residual Learning@@3.1 残差学习
P@@Let us consider H(x) as an underlying mapping to be fit by a few stacked layers, with x denoting the inputs to the first of these layers. If one hypothesizes that multiple nonlinear layers can asymptotically approximate complicated functions, then it is equivalent to hypothesize that they can asymptotically approximate the residual functions, i.e., H(x) − x (assuming that the input and output are of the same dimensions). So rather than expect stacked layers to approximate H(x), we explicitly let these layers approximate a residual function F(x) := H(x) − x. The original function thus becomes F(x) + x. Although both forms should be able to asymptotically approximate the desired functions, the ease of learning might be different.@@考虑由几层堆叠层拟合的目标映射 H(x)，x 表示这些层中第一层的输入。若假设多个非线性层能渐近逼近复杂函数，那么等价于假设它们能渐近逼近残差函数 H(x) − x（假设输入输出同维）。因此，我们不让堆叠层去逼近 H(x)，而是明确让它们逼近残差函数 F(x) := H(x) − x，原函数由此变成 F(x) + x。虽然两种形式理论上都能渐近逼近目标函数，但<strong>学习的容易程度可能不同</strong>。
P@@This reformulation is motivated by the counterintuitive phenomena about the degradation problem. As discussed in the introduction, if the added layers can be constructed as identity mappings, a deeper model should have training error no greater than its shallower counterpart. The degradation problem suggests that the solvers might have difficulties in approximating identity mappings by multiple nonlinear layers. With the residual learning reformulation, if identity mappings are optimal, the solvers may simply drive the weights of the multiple nonlinear layers toward zero to approach identity mappings.@@这种重新表述的动机来自退化问题的反直觉现象。如引言所述，若新增层可构造为恒等映射，更深模型的训练误差不应高于浅层对应模型。退化问题表明，求解器可能难以用多个非线性层逼近恒等映射。有了残差学习表述，若恒等映射是最优的，求解器只需把多个非线性层的权重推向零，即可逼近恒等映射。
P@@In real cases, it is unlikely that identity mappings are optimal, but our reformulation may help to precondition the problem. If the optimal function is closer to an identity mapping than to a zero mapping, it should be easier for the solver to find the perturbations with reference to an identity mapping, than to learn the function as a new one. We show by experiments that the learned residual functions in general have small responses, suggesting that identity mappings provide reasonable preconditioning.@@在真实情形中，恒等映射不太可能是最优的，但我们的表述有助于为问题做预条件。若最优函数更接近恒等映射而非零映射，那么求解器以恒等映射为参照寻找扰动，应比从零学一个新函数更容易。实验表明，学到的残差函数通常响应值很小，这说明恒等映射提供了合理的预条件。
SUB@@3.2 Identity Mapping by Shortcuts@@3.2 通过短路实现恒等映射
P@@We adopt residual learning to every few stacked layers. Formally, we consider a building block defined as y = F(x, {W_i}) + x. Here x and y are the input and output vectors of the layers considered. The function F(x, {W_i}) represents the residual mapping to be learned.@@我们对每几层堆叠层采用残差学习。形式化地，考虑一个定义为 y = F(x, {Wᵢ}) + x 的构件块。这里 x 和 y 是所考虑层的输入与输出向量，函数 F(x, {Wᵢ}) 表示要学习的残差映射。
FIG@@fig2_residual.png@@Figure 2. Residual learning: a building block.@@图 2 · 残差学习：一个构件块。@@这张图值得反复看：x 有两条路——一条穿过两层权重+ReLU 得到 F(x)，另一条恒等短路直接把 x 送到加法处；相加后再过一个 ReLU。<b>短路那一笔只是"把输入原样接过来"，不引入任何参数或计算量</b>，却是整个 ResNet 的灵魂。
EQ@@y = F(x, {W<sub>i</sub>}) + x &nbsp;&nbsp;...(1)<br>y = F(x, {W<sub>i</sub>}) + W<sub>s</sub>x &nbsp;&nbsp;...(2)@@(1,2)@@式 (1) 是恒等短路（x、F 维度相同）；式 (2) 在维度变化时用 1×1 卷积投影 W_s 匹配维度。对两层的例子，F = W₂σ(W₁x)（σ 为 ReLU，偏置省略）。实验表明恒等短路已足够解决退化问题，W_s 只在需要匹配维度时使用。
P@@The operation F + x is performed by a shortcut connection and element-wise addition. We adopt the second nonlinearity after the addition (i.e., σ(y)). The shortcut connections introduce neither extra parameter nor computation complexity. This is not only attractive in practice but also important in our comparisons between plain and residual networks — we can fairly compare networks that have the same number of parameters, depth, width, and computational cost.@@F + x 的操作由短路连接与逐元素相加实现。我们在相加之后采用第二个非线性（即 σ(y)）。短路连接既不引入额外参数，也不引入计算复杂度。这不仅在实践中有吸引力，在我们对 plain 与残差网络的比较中也很重要——我们可以公平地比较参数量、深度、宽度和计算成本都相同的网络。
NOTE@@<b>公平对比是这篇论文实验设计的高明之处</b>：plain 和 residual 网络参数量、深度、宽度完全相同，唯一差别就是有没有那条短路。所以性能差异可以干净地归因于"残差结构"本身，而不是"参数更多"。做实验设计时值得学习这种控制变量法。
P@@The dimensions of x and F must be equal in Eqn.(1). If this is not the case (e.g., when changing the input/output channels), we can perform a linear projection W_s by the shortcut connections to match the dimensions. We can also use a square matrix W_s in Eqn.(1). But we will show by experiments that the identity mapping is sufficient for addressing the degradation problem and is economical, and thus W_s is only used when matching dimensions.@@式 (1) 中 x 与 F 的维度必须相等。若非如此（例如改变输入输出通道数），可通过短路连接做线性投影 W_s 来匹配维度。我们也可以在式 (1) 中用方阵 W_s，但实验表明恒等映射已足以解决退化问题且更经济，因此 W_s 只在匹配维度时使用。
P@@The form of the residual function F is flexible. Experiments involve F with two or three layers, while more layers are possible. But if F has only a single layer, Eqn.(1) is similar to a linear layer y = W₁x + x, for which we have not observed advantages. Although the above notations are about fully-connected layers for simplicity, they are applicable to convolutional layers. The element-wise addition is performed on two feature maps, channel by channel.@@残差函数 F 的形式是灵活的。实验中的 F 有两层或三层，更多层也可能。但若 F 只有单层，式 (1) 就类似于线性层 y = W₁x + x，我们没有观察到优势。虽然上述记号为简洁起见是关于全连接层的，但它们也适用于卷积层——逐元素相加在两个特征图上按通道进行。
SUB@@3.3 Network Architectures@@3.3 网络架构
P@@Plain Network: Our plain baselines are mainly inspired by the philosophy of VGG nets. The convolutional layers mostly have 3×3 filters and follow two simple design rules: (i) for the same output feature map size, the layers have the same number of filters; (ii) if the feature map size is halved, the number of filters is doubled so as to preserve the time complexity per layer. We perform downsampling directly by convolutional layers that have a stride of 2. The network ends with a global average pooling layer and a 1000-way fully-connected layer with softmax.@@Plain 网络：我们的 plain 基线主要受 VGG 网络理念启发。卷积层多用 3×3 滤波器，遵循两条简单设计规则：(i) 输出特征图尺寸相同的层，滤波器数量相同；(ii) 若特征图尺寸减半，滤波器数量翻倍，以保持每层的时间复杂度。我们直接用步长为 2 的卷积层做下采样。网络以全局平均池化层和带 softmax 的 1000 路全连接层结束。
P@@It is worth noticing that our model has fewer filters and lower complexity than VGG nets. Our 34-layer baseline has 3.6 billion FLOPs (multiply-adds), which is only 18% of VGG-19 (19.6 billion FLOPs).@@值得注意的是，我们的模型比 VGG 网络滤波器更少、复杂度更低。34 层基线有 36 亿 FLOPs（乘加运算），仅为 VGG-19（196 亿 FLOPs）的 18%。
P@@Residual Network: Based on the above plain network, we insert shortcut connections which turn the network into its counterpart residual version. The identity shortcuts can be directly used when the input and output are of the same dimensions. When the dimensions increase, we consider two options: (A) The shortcut still performs identity mapping, with extra zero entries padded for increasing dimensions. This option introduces no extra parameter; (B) The projection shortcut is used to match dimensions (done by 1×1 convolutions). For both options, when the shortcuts go across feature maps of two sizes, they are performed with a stride of 2.@@残差网络：在上述 plain 网络基础上，我们插入短路连接，把网络变成对应的残差版本。输入输出同维时直接用恒等短路。维度增加时考虑两种方案：(A) 短路仍做恒等映射，对增加的维度补零，不引入额外参数；(B) 用式 (2) 的投影短路匹配维度（由 1×1 卷积完成）。两种方案中，当短路跨越两种尺寸的特征图时，都以步长 2 执行。
SUB@@3.4 Implementation@@3.4 实现细节
P@@Our implementation follows prior practice: the image is resized with its shorter side randomly sampled in [256, 480] for scale augmentation. A 224×224 crop is randomly sampled from an image or its horizontal flip, with the per-pixel mean subtracted. We adopt batch normalization right after each convolution and before activation. We train all plain/residual nets from scratch with SGD, mini-batch size 256, learning rate starting from 0.1 and divided by 10 when the error plateaus, weight decay 0.0001, momentum 0.9, and no dropout.@@实现遵循已有实践：图像短边在 [256, 480] 中随机采样以实现尺度增强；从图像或其水平翻转中随机采 224×224 裁剪，减去逐像素均值；每次卷积后、激活前采用 BatchNorm。所有 plain/残差网络都从头训练：SGD，批大小 256，学习率从 0.1 开始、误差停滞时除以 10，权重衰减 0.0001，动量 0.9，不使用 dropout。
NOTE@@注意"不使用 dropout"：作者刻意在 plain 与残差之间保持相同的训练配置，以隔离结构差异。而且 BN 已经承担了一部分正则作用。今天训练 ResNet 基本沿用这套配置（SGD + momentum + step decay + BN + no dropout）。

CH@@5@@5. Conclusions@@5. 结论
LEAD@@论文正文到此结束（之后是附录检测细节）。结论重申残差结构的通用性——它不仅解决视觉任务，也预期适用于"其它视觉与非视觉问题"。这句话在 2017 年后被 Transformer 与 LLM 完全印证。
P@@This paper presented a residual learning framework that addresses the degradation problem in very deep networks. The key idea is to let layers learn residual functions with reference to their inputs, realized by parameter-free identity shortcut connections. This formulation makes deep networks easy to optimize and enables accuracy gains from increased depth.@@本文提出一个残差学习框架，解决极深网络中的退化问题。核心思想是让各层以输入为参照学习残差函数，通过无参数的恒等短路连接实现。这一表述使深度网络易于优化，并能从增加的深度中获得精度收益。
P@@The 152-layer residual net achieved state-of-the-art results on ImageNet with lower complexity than VGG nets, and the method won first places in five tracks of ILSVRC & COCO 2015 competitions.
