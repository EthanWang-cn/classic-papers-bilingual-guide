# ============ Abstract ============
CH@@abstract@@Abstract@@摘要
LEAD@@摘要给出三个层次的信号：方法（双向预训练）、用法（加一层输出头微调）、战绩（11 项任务 SOTA）。但最该留意的是那句 <em>jointly conditioning on both left and right context in all layers</em>——「每一层都联合左右上下文」，这是 BERT 与 ELMo（浅层拼接）的分水岭。
P@@We introduce a new language representation model called BERT, which stands for Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. Unlike recent language representation models (Peters et al., 2018a; Radford et al., 2018), BERT is designed to pre-train deep bidirectional representations from unlabeled text by jointly conditioning on both left and right context in all layers.@@我们提出一种新的语言表示模型，名为 BERT，即 <strong>Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers</strong>（基于 Transformer 的双向编码器表示）。与近期的语言表示模型（Peters 等，2018a；Radford 等，2018）不同，BERT 的设计目标是：在<strong>所有层</strong>中联合地以左侧与右侧上下文为条件，从无标注文本中预训练深层双向表示。
P@@As a result, the pre-trained BERT model can be fine-tuned with just one additional output layer to create state-of-the-art models for a wide range of tasks, such as question answering and language inference, without substantial task-specific architecture modifications.@@因此，预训练好的 BERT 模型只需<strong>再加一个输出层</strong>进行微调，就能为问答、语言推理等广泛任务构建最先进的模型，而无需对特定任务的架构做实质性修改。
NOTE@@「just one additional output layer」（只需一个额外输出层）是这篇论文的工程美学：所有任务共用同一个 backbone，差异被压缩到最后一两层。<b>这正是今天「基础模型 + 下游适配」范式的原型</b>——从 LoRA 到 prompt tuning，都在延续这个思路。
P@@BERT is conceptually simple and empirically powerful. It obtains new state-of-the-art results on eleven natural language processing tasks, including pushing the GLUE score to 80.5% (7.7% point absolute improvement), MultiNLI accuracy to 86.7% (4.6% absolute improvement), SQuAD v1.1 question answering Test F1 to 93.2 (1.5 point absolute improvement) and SQuAD v2.0 Test F1 to 83.1 (5.1 point absolute improvement).@@BERT 在概念上简单，在实证上强大。它在 11 项自然语言处理任务上取得新的最先进结果，包括：将 GLUE 分数提升至 80.5%（绝对提升 7.7 个百分点）、MultiNLI 准确率提升至 86.7%（绝对提升 4.6 个百分点）、SQuAD v1.1 问答测试 F1 提升至 93.2（绝对提升 1.5 点）、SQuAD v2.0 测试 F1 提升至 83.1（绝对提升 5.1 点）。
NOTE@@注意这些提升发生在 2018 年底：GLUE +7.7、SQuAD 2.0 +5.1 都是<b>断层式</b>的领先，而不是常规迭代。这种「一篇论文重写整个榜单」的冲击力，是它被称作 NLP 的 ImageNet 时刻的原因。
ENDCH

# ============ 1 Introduction ============
CH@@1@@1. Introduction@@1. 引言
LEAD@@引言要回答一个问题：既然预训练已被证明有效，为什么还要 BERT？作者的答案是——<strong>现有方法都被「单向」绑住了</strong>。GPT 只能看左边，ELMo 虽有两个方向但只是浅层拼接。要释放双向的威力，必须换一个预训练目标。
P@@Language model pre-training has been shown to be effective for improving many natural language processing tasks (Dai and Le, 2015; Peters et al., 2018a; Radford et al., 2018; Howard and Ruder, 2018). These include sentence-level tasks such as natural language inference (Bowman et al., 2015; Williams et al., 2018) and paraphrasing (Dolan and Brockett, 2005), which aim to predict the relationships between sentences by analyzing them holistically, as well as token-level tasks such as named entity recognition and question answering, where models are required to produce fine-grained output at the token level (Tjong Kim Sang and De Meulder, 2003; Rajpurkar et al., 2016).@@语言模型预训练已被证明能有效改善许多自然语言处理任务（Dai 和 Le，2015；Peters 等，2018a；Radford 等，2018；Howard 和 Ruder，2018）。这些任务包括<strong>句子级任务</strong>，如自然语言推理（Bowman 等，2015；Williams 等，2018）和复述识别（Dolan 和 Brockett，2005）——它们需要通过整体分析来预测句子之间的关系；也包括<strong>词元级任务</strong>，如命名实体识别和问答——模型需要在词元粒度上产生细粒度输出（Tjong Kim Sang 和 De Meulder，2003；Rajpurkar 等，2016）。
P@@There are two existing strategies for applying pre-trained language representations to downstream tasks: feature-based and fine-tuning. The feature-based approach, such as ELMo (Peters et al., 2018a), uses task-specific architectures that include the pre-trained representations as additional features. The fine-tuning approach, such as the Generative Pre-trained Transformer (OpenAI GPT) (Radford et al., 2018), introduces minimal task-specific parameters, and is trained on the downstream tasks by simply fine-tuning all pre-trained parameters. The two approaches share the same objective function during pre-training, where they use unidirectional language models to learn general language representations.@@将预训练语言表示用于下游任务，现有两种策略：<strong>特征式</strong>与<strong>微调式</strong>。特征式方法（如 ELMo，Peters 等，2018a）使用特定任务的架构，把预训练表示作为附加特征纳入其中。微调式方法（如生成式预训练 Transformer，OpenAI GPT，Radford 等，2018）只引入极少的任务专用参数，通过简单地微调所有预训练参数来在下游任务上训练。两种方法在预训练阶段共用同一个目标函数：都使用<strong>单向语言模型</strong>来学习通用语言表示。
NOTE@@这句话是全文的「靶子」：**两种主流路线虽然用法不同，但预训练目标都是单向 LM**。作者要攻击的不是 ELMo 或 GPT 的某个技巧，而是它们共同的这个前提。
P@@We argue that current techniques restrict the power of the pre-trained representations, especially for the fine-tuning approaches. The major limitation is that standard language models are unidirectional, and this limits the choice of architectures that can be used during pre-training. For example, in OpenAI GPT, the authors use a left-to-right architecture, where every token can only attend to previous tokens in the self-attention layers of the Transformer (Vaswani et al., 2017). Such restrictions are sub-optimal for sentence-level tasks, and could be very harmful when applying fine-tuning based approaches to token-level tasks such as question answering, where it is crucial to incorporate context from both directions.@@我们认为，现有技术限制了预训练表示的能力，对微调式方法尤甚。主要局限在于：标准语言模型是<strong>单向的</strong>，这限制了预训练时可选用的架构。例如，OpenAI GPT 采用从左到右的架构，其中每个词元在 Transformer 的自注意力层中只能关注它之前的词元（Vaswani 等，2017）。这种限制对句子级任务而言是次优的；而当把微调式方法用于问答等词元级任务时，可能非常有害——因为这些任务关键在于<strong>同时纳入两个方向的上下文</strong>。
P@@In this paper, we improve the fine-tuning based approaches by proposing BERT: Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. BERT alleviates the previously mentioned unidirectionality constraint by using a "masked language model" (MLM) pre-training objective, inspired by the Cloze task (Taylor, 1953). The masked language model randomly masks some of the tokens from the input, and the objective is to predict the original vocabulary id of the masked word based only on its context.@@本文通过提出 BERT 来改进微调式方法。BERT 借助受完形填空任务（Cloze task，Taylor，1953）启发的<strong>掩码语言模型</strong>（masked language model，MLM）预训练目标，缓解了上述单向性约束。掩码语言模型随机遮盖输入中的一些词元，目标是仅根据其上下文来预测被遮盖词的原始词表编号。
NOTE@@「完形填空」这个比喻值得记住：**单向 LM 是「续写」，MLM 是「填空」**。续写只能靠上文；填空天然要同时看左右。一个目标函数的替换，就把架构从单向解放成双向——这是全篇最漂亮的一招。
P@@Unlike left-to-right language model pre-training, the MLM objective enables the representation to fuse the left and the right context, which allows us to pre-train a deep bidirectional Transformer. In addition to the masked language model, we also use a "next sentence prediction" task that jointly pre-trains text-pair representations. The contributions of our paper are as follows:@@与从左到右的语言模型预训练不同，MLM 目标使表示能够<strong>融合左右两侧的上下文</strong>，从而让我们可以预训练一个深层双向 Transformer。除掩码语言模型之外，我们还使用了「<strong>下一句预测</strong>」任务，联合预训练文本对表示。本文贡献如下：
P@@• We demonstrate the importance of bidirectional pre-training for language representations. Unlike Radford et al. (2018), which uses unidirectional language models for pre-training, BERT uses masked language models to enable pre-trained deep bidirectional representations. This is also in contrast to Peters et al. (2018a), which uses a shallow concatenation of independently trained left-to-right and right-to-left LMs.@@• 我们证明了<strong>双向预训练</strong>对语言表示的重要性。与 Radford 等（2018）使用单向语言模型不同，BERT 使用掩码语言模型，得到预训练的深层双向表示。这也不同于 Peters 等（2018a）——后者把独立训练的左到右与右到左语言模型做<strong>浅层拼接</strong>。
P@@• We show that pre-trained representations reduce the need for many heavily-engineered task-specific architectures. BERT is the first fine-tuning based representation model that achieves state-of-the-art performance on a large suite of sentence-level and token-level tasks, outperforming many task-specific architectures.@@• 我们表明，预训练表示<strong>减少了对大量精心设计的任务专用架构的需求</strong>。BERT 是首个在大规模句子级与词元级任务套件上都达到最先进性能的微调式表示模型，优于许多任务专用架构。
P@@• BERT advances the state of the art for eleven NLP tasks. The code and pre-trained models are available at https://github.com/google-research/bert.@@• BERT 推进了 11 项 NLP 任务的最先进水平。代码与预训练模型见 https://github.com/google-research/bert。
ENDCH

# ============ 2 Related Work ============
CH@@2@@2. Related Work@@2. 相关工作
LEAD@@这节梳理「预训练表示」的谱系：词嵌入 → 句嵌入 → ELMo（上下文相关）→ GPT（微调式）→ 监督迁移。读它只需抓住一条线：大家都在做预训练，但<strong>没有人做到「深层双向 + 微调」</strong>。
SUB@@2.1 Unsupervised Feature-based Approaches@@2.1 无监督特征式方法
P@@Learning widely applicable representations of words has been an active area of research for decades, including non-neural (Brown et al., 1992; Ando and Zhang, 2005; Blitzer et al., 2006) and neural (Mikolov et al., 2013; Pennington et al., 2014) methods. Pre-trained word embeddings are an integral part of modern NLP systems, offering significant improvements over embeddings learned from scratch (Turian et al., 2010). To pre-train word embedding vectors, left-to-right language modeling objectives have been used (Mnih and Hinton, 2009), as well as objectives to discriminate correct from incorrect words in left and right context (Mikolov et al., 2013).@@学习广泛适用的词表示数十年来一直是活跃研究领域，包括非神经方法（Brown 等，1992；Ando 和 Zhang，2005；Blitzer 等，2006）与神经方法（Mikolov 等，2013；Pennington 等，2014）。预训练词嵌入是现代 NLP 系统不可或缺的组成部分，相比从头学习的嵌入带来显著改进（Turian 等，2010）。预训练词嵌入向量的方法包括：从左到右的语言建模目标（Mnih 和 Hinton，2009），以及在左右上下文中区分正确词与错误词的目标（Mikolov 等，2013）。
P@@These approaches have been generalized to coarser granularities, such as sentence embeddings (Kiros et al., 2015; Logeswaran and Lee, 2018) or paragraph embeddings (Le and Mikolov, 2014). To train sentence representations, prior work has used objectives to rank candidate next sentences (Jernite et al., 2017; Logeswaran and Lee, 2018), left-to-right generation of next sentence words given a representation of the previous sentence (Kiros et al., 2015), or denoising auto-encoder derived objectives (Hill et al., 2016).@@这些方法已被推广到更粗的粒度，如句子嵌入（Kiros 等，2015；Logeswaran 和 Lee，2018）或段落嵌入（Le 和 Mikolov，2014）。为训练句子表示，已有工作使用了如下目标：对候选下一句进行排序（Jernite 等，2017；Logeswaran 和 Lee，2018）；在给定上一句表示的情况下从左到右生成下一句的词（Kiros 等，2015）；或由去噪自编码器导出的目标（Hill 等，2016）。
P@@ELMo and its predecessor (Peters et al., 2017, 2018a) generalize traditional word embedding research along a different dimension. They extract context-sensitive features from a left-to-right and a right-to-left language model. The contextual representation of each token is the concatenation of the left-to-right and right-to-left representations. When integrating contextual word embeddings with existing task-specific architectures, ELMo advances the state of the art for several major NLP benchmarks (Peters et al., 2018a) including question answering (Rajpurkar et al., 2016), sentiment analysis (Socher et al., 2013), and named entity recognition (Tjong Kim Sang and De Meulder, 2003). Melamud et al. (2016) proposed learning contextual representations through a task to predict a single word from both left and right context using LSTMs. Similar to ELMo, their model is feature-based and not deeply bidirectional. Fedus et al. (2018) shows that the cloze task can be used to improve the robustness of text generation models.@@ELMo 及其前身（Peters 等，2017，2018a）沿另一个维度推广了传统词嵌入研究。它们从一个左到右语言模型和一个右到左语言模型中提取<strong>上下文敏感的特征</strong>，每个词元的上下文表示是左到右与右到左表示的<strong>拼接</strong>。当把上下文词嵌入与现有任务专用架构结合时，ELMo 在多个重要 NLP 基准上推进了最先进水平（Peters 等，2018a），包括问答（Rajpurkar 等，2016）、情感分析（Socher 等，2013）与命名实体识别（Tjong Kim Sang 和 De Meulder，2003）。Melamud 等（2016）提出用 LSTM 从左右上下文预测单个词的任务来学习上下文表示。与 ELMo 类似，他们的模型是特征式的，且<strong>不是深层双向</strong>。Fedus 等（2018）表明完形填空任务可用于提升文本生成模型的鲁棒性。
NOTE@@注意作者对 ELMo 的精确措辞：<b>「shallow concatenation」（浅层拼接）</b>。ELMo 的左右两个 LSTM 互不通信，只在最后把结果拼起来；每个词在第 k 层并没有同时看到左右。BERT 的全部主张就是「深层联合条件化」——这个区别在图 3 里画得非常清楚。
SUB@@2.2 Unsupervised Fine-tuning Approaches@@2.2 无监督微调式方法
P@@As with the feature-based approaches, the first works in this direction only pre-trained word embedding parameters from unlabeled text (Collobert and Weston, 2008). More recently, sentence or document encoders which produce contextual token representations have been pre-trained from unlabeled text and fine-tuned for a supervised downstream task (Dai and Le, 2015; Howard and Ruder, 2018; Radford et al., 2018). The advantage of these approaches is that few parameters need to be learned from scratch. At least partly due to this advantage, OpenAI GPT (Radford et al., 2018) achieved previously state-of-the-art results on many sentence-level tasks from the GLUE benchmark (Wang et al., 2018a). Left-to-right language modeling and auto-encoder objectives have been used for pre-training such models (Howard and Ruder, 2018; Radford et al., 2018; Dai and Le, 2015).@@与特征式方法类似，这一方向的最早工作只从无标注文本中预训练词嵌入参数（Collobert 和 Weston，2008）。近期，能产生上下文词元表示的句子或文档编码器被提出：先从无标注文本预训练，再针对有监督的下游任务微调（Dai 和 Le，2015；Howard 和 Ruder，2018；Radford 等，2018）。这些方法的优势在于：<strong>只有很少的参数需要从头学习</strong>。至少部分由于这一优势，OpenAI GPT（Radford 等，2018）在 GLUE 基准的许多句子级任务上取得了当时的最先进结果（Wang 等，2018a）。预训练这类模型使用了从左到右语言建模与自编码器目标（Howard 和 Ruder，2018；Radford 等，2018；Dai 和 Le，2015）。
SUB@@2.3 Transfer Learning from Supervised Data@@2.3 来自监督数据的迁移学习
P@@There has also been work showing effective transfer from supervised tasks with large datasets, such as natural language inference (Conneau et al., 2017) and machine translation (McCann et al., 2017). Computer vision research has also demonstrated the importance of transfer learning from large pre-trained models, where an effective recipe is to fine-tune models pre-trained with ImageNet (Deng et al., 2009; Yosinski et al., 2014).@@也有工作表明，从拥有大规模数据集的监督任务（如自然语言推理，Conneau 等，2017；机器翻译，McCann 等，2017）出发能有效迁移。计算机视觉研究同样证明了从大型预训练模型迁移的重要性，其中一个有效配方是：微调在 ImageNet 上预训练的模型（Deng 等，2009；Yosinski 等，2014）。
NOTE@@<b>「ImageNet 配方」在这里被明确点名</b>。BERT 的作者是在有意识地把 CV 的成功范式搬到 NLP：大数据预训练 → 小数据微调。导读部分会展开这个类比为什么成立、又有什么不同。
ENDCH

# ============ 3 BERT ============
CH@@3@@3. BERT@@3. BERT 模型
LEAD@@全文技术核心。先把两件事分清：<strong>架构</strong>（就是一个标准 Transformer 编码器，没有新发明）与<strong>预训练目标</strong>（MLM + NSP，这才是创新）。读懂 3.1 的两个任务，就读懂了 BERT 的 90%。
P@@We introduce BERT and its detailed implementation in this section. There are two steps in our framework: pre-training and fine-tuning. During pre-training, the model is trained on unlabeled data over different pre-training tasks. For fine-tuning, the BERT model is first initialized with the pre-trained parameters, and all of the parameters are fine-tuned using labeled data from the downstream tasks. Each downstream task has separate fine-tuned models, even though they are initialized with the same pre-trained parameters. The question-answering example in Figure 1 will serve as a running example for this section.@@本节介绍 BERT 及其具体实现。我们的框架包含两步：<strong>预训练</strong>与<strong>微调</strong>。预训练阶段，模型在无标注数据上通过不同预训练任务训练。微调阶段，BERT 模型先用预训练参数初始化，再用下游任务的标注数据微调<strong>所有参数</strong>。尽管各下游任务都由同一份预训练参数初始化，但它们各自得到独立的微调模型。本节将以图 1 中的问答示例贯穿始终。
FIG@@fig1_pretrain_finetune.png@@Figure 1. Overall pre-training and fine-tuning procedures for BERT. Apart from output layers, the same architectures are used in both pre-training and fine-tuning. The same pre-trained model parameters are used to initialize models for different down-stream tasks. During fine-tuning, all parameters are fine-tuned. [CLS] is a special symbol added in front of every input example, and [SEP] is a special separator token (e.g. separating questions/answers).@@图 1 · BERT 的预训练与微调整体流程。除输出层外，预训练与微调使用相同的架构；同一份预训练参数用于初始化不同下游任务的模型，微调时所有参数都会被更新。[CLS] 是加在每个输入样本前的特殊符号，[SEP] 是分隔符（例如分隔问题与答案）。@@这张图是整篇论文的「总装图」：左边预训练（Masked Sentence A/B → BERT → NSP + Mask LM），右边微调（Question/Paragraph → 同一个 BERT → Start/End Span）。注意两条路径的 BERT 盒子长得完全一样——**这正是 BERT 的核心卖点：一个模型，多种任务**。
P@@A distinctive feature of BERT is its unified architecture across different tasks. There is minimal difference between the pre-trained architecture and the final downstream architecture.@@BERT 的一个显著特点是：<strong>跨任务架构统一</strong>。预训练架构与最终下游架构之间几乎没有差别。
H4@@Model Architecture@@模型架构
P@@BERT's model architecture is a multi-layer bidirectional Transformer encoder based on the original implementation described in Vaswani et al. (2017) and released in the tensor2tensor library. Because the use of Transformers has become common and our implementation is almost identical to the original, we will omit an exhaustive background description of the model architecture and refer readers to Vaswani et al. (2017) as well as excellent guides such as "The Annotated Transformer."@@BERT 的模型架构是一个<strong>多层双向 Transformer 编码器</strong>，基于 Vaswani 等（2017）描述并在 tensor2tensor 库中发布的原始实现。由于 Transformer 的使用已很普遍，且我们的实现与原始版本几乎相同，我们省略对模型架构的详尽背景描述，建议读者参考 Vaswani 等（2017）以及《The Annotated Transformer》等优秀指南。
NOTE@@这句话把 BERT 的血统说得很清楚：**BERT 没有发明新架构，它用的是 Transformer 的编码器部分**。上一篇论文（Attention Is All You Need）在这里直接成为基础设施。把本仓库第 001 篇与本篇连起来读，因果链非常清晰。
P@@In this work, we denote the number of layers (i.e., Transformer blocks) as L, the hidden size as H, and the number of self-attention heads as A. We primarily report results on two model sizes: BERT<sub>BASE</sub> (L=12, H=768, A=12, Total Parameters=110M) and BERT<sub>LARGE</sub> (L=24, H=1024, A=16, Total Parameters=340M). BERT<sub>BASE</sub> was chosen to have the same model size as OpenAI GPT for comparison purposes. Critically, however, the BERT Transformer uses bidirectional self-attention, while the GPT Transformer uses constrained self-attention where every token can only attend to context to its left.@@本文中，我们把层数（即 Transformer 块数）记为 L，隐层大小记为 H，自注意力头数记为 A。我们主要报告两种模型规模的结果：<strong>BERT<sub>BASE</sub></strong>（L=12，H=768，A=12，总参数 1.1 亿）与 <strong>BERT<sub>LARGE</sub></strong>（L=24，H=1024，A=16，总参数 3.4 亿）。选择 BERT<sub>BASE</sub> 是为了使其规模与 OpenAI GPT 相同，便于比较。但关键区别在于：BERT 的 Transformer 使用<strong>双向自注意力</strong>，而 GPT 的 Transformer 使用<strong>受限自注意力</strong>——每个词元只能关注其左侧上下文。

H4@@Task #1: Masked LM@@任务一：掩码语言模型
P@@Intuitively, it is reasonable to believe that a deep bidirectional model is strictly more powerful than either a left-to-right model or the shallow concatenation of a left-to-right and a right-to-left model. Unfortunately, standard conditional language models can only be trained left-to-right or right-to-left, since bidirectional conditioning would allow each word to indirectly "see itself", and the model could trivially predict the target word in a multi-layered context.@@直觉上，有理由相信深层双向模型严格强于从左到右模型，也强于左到右与右到左模型的浅层拼接。遗憾的是，标准条件语言模型只能从左到右或从右到左训练，因为<strong>双向条件化会让每个词间接「看见自己」</strong>，模型便能在多层上下文中轻而易举地预测目标词。
NOTE@@<b>这是全篇最关键的因果论证</b>：为什么以前不做双向？不是没想到，而是「双向 + 预测自己」会让任务退化为抄答案。MLM 的聪明之处在于把要预测的词<b>先盖住</b>——它不在输入里出现，模型想抄也抄不到。一句话解开了一个死结。
P@@In order to train a deep bidirectional representation, we simply mask some percentage of the input tokens at random, and then predict those masked tokens. We refer to this procedure as a "masked LM" (MLM), although it is often referred to as a Cloze task in the literature (Taylor, 1953). In this case, the final hidden vectors corresponding to the mask tokens are fed into an output softmax over the vocabulary, as in a standard LM. In all of our experiments, we mask 15% of all WordPiece tokens in each sequence at random. In contrast to denoising auto-encoders (Vincent et al., 2008), we only predict the masked words rather than reconstructing the entire input.@@为训练深层双向表示，我们只需随机遮盖一定比例的输入词元，然后预测这些被遮盖的词元。我们把这一过程称为<strong>掩码语言模型</strong>（masked LM，MLM），文献中也常称为完形填空任务（Cloze task，Taylor，1953）。此时，与被遮盖词元对应的最后隐向量被送入词表上的输出 softmax，与标准语言模型一样。在所有实验中，我们随机遮盖每个序列中 <strong>15%</strong> 的 WordPiece 词元。与去噪自编码器（Vincent 等，2008）不同，我们只预测被遮盖的词，而<strong>不重构整个输入</strong>。
P@@Although this allows us to obtain a bidirectional pre-trained model, a downside is that we are creating a mismatch between pre-training and fine-tuning, since the [MASK] token does not appear during fine-tuning. To mitigate this, we do not always replace "masked" words with the actual [MASK] token. The training data generator chooses 15% of the token positions at random for prediction. If the i-th token is chosen, we replace the i-th token with (1) the [MASK] token 80% of the time (2) a random token 10% of the time (3) the unchanged i-th token 10% of the time. Then, T<sub>i</sub> will be used to predict the original token with cross entropy loss. We compare variations of this procedure in Appendix C.2.@@虽然这让我们得到双向预训练模型，但代价是造成了<strong>预训练与微调之间的不匹配</strong>：因为 [MASK] 词元在微调阶段并不出现。为缓解这一点，我们并不总是把「被遮盖」的词替换为真正的 [MASK] 词元。训练数据生成器随机选择 15% 的词元位置用于预测；若第 i 个词元被选中，则按如下方式替换：(1) <strong>80%</strong> 的概率替换为 [MASK] 词元；(2) <strong>10%</strong> 的概率替换为一个随机词元；(3) <strong>10%</strong> 的概率保持原词不变。随后用 T<sub>i</sub> 以交叉熵损失预测原始词元。我们在附录 C.2 中比较了这一过程的各种变体。
NOTE@@<b>80/10/10 是 BERT 最容易被跳过、却最值得琢磨的细节</b>。如果 15% 全用 [MASK]，模型在微调时从未见过这个符号，表示就会「偏科」；掺入 10% 随机词和 10% 原词，迫使模型<b>对每一个位置都保持好的上下文表示</b>——因为它不知道哪个位置会被考到。附录表 8 用实验证明了这个设计确实有用，尤其在特征式用法下。
H4@@Task #2: Next Sentence Prediction (NSP)@@任务二：下一句预测（NSP）
P@@Many important downstream tasks such as Question Answering (QA) and Natural Language Inference (NLI) are based on understanding the relationship between two sentences, which is not directly captured by language modeling. In order to train a model that understands sentence relationships, we pre-train for a binarized next sentence prediction task that can be trivially generated from any monolingual corpus. Specifically, when choosing the sentences A and B for each pre-training example, 50% of the time B is the actual next sentence that follows A (labeled as IsNext), and 50% of the time it is a random sentence from the corpus (labeled as NotNext).@@许多重要下游任务，如问答（QA）与自然语言推理（NLI），都建立在<strong>理解两个句子之间的关系</strong>之上，而这无法被语言建模直接捕捉。为训练一个理解句子关系的模型，我们预训练了一个二值化的<strong>下一句预测</strong>任务，它可以从任何单语语料库中轻易生成。具体而言，为每个预训练样本选择句子 A 和 B 时，50% 的情况下 B 是紧接 A 的真实下一句（标记为 IsNext），另外 50% 的情况下 B 是语料库中的一个随机句子（标记为 NotNext）。
P@@As we show in Figure 1, C is used for next sentence prediction (NSP). Despite its simplicity, we demonstrate in Section 5.1 that pre-training towards this task is very beneficial to both QA and NLI.@@如图 1 所示，C（即 [CLS] 位置的表示）被用于下一句预测（NSP）。尽管这个任务很简单，我们在 5.1 节证明：针对它做预训练对 QA 和 NLI 都非常有益。
NOTE@@<b>需要在导读里诚实指出</b>：后来的 RoBERTa（2019）证明 NSP 其实贡献有限，去掉它反而更好。所以 NSP 是 BERT 的「时代补丁」，不是永恒洞见。但它在当时确实提供了「句子对关系」这一训练信号，也塑造了 [CLS] 的语义。区分「核心贡献」与「历史局限」，正是精读的价值。
H4@@Pre-training Data@@预训练数据
P@@The pre-training procedure largely follows the existing literature on language model pre-training. For the pre-training corpus we use the BooksCorpus (800M words) (Zhu et al., 2015) and English Wikipedia (2,500M words). For Wikipedia we extract only the text passages and ignore lists, tables, and headers. It is critical to use a document-level corpus rather than a shuffled sentence-level corpus such as the Billion Word Benchmark (Chelba et al., 2013) in order to extract long contiguous sequences.@@预训练流程基本遵循语言模型预训练的已有文献。预训练语料使用 <strong>BooksCorpus</strong>（8 亿词，Zhu 等，2015）与<strong>英文维基百科</strong>（25 亿词）。对维基百科，我们只抽取正文段落，忽略列表、表格和标题。必须使用<strong>文档级语料</strong>而非像 Billion Word Benchmark（Chelba 等，2013）那样被打乱的句子级语料，这一点至关重要——这样才能抽取长的连续序列。
NOTE@@「document-level corpus」这个要求常被忽略。因为 NSP 需要真实的相邻句关系，MLM 也需要长上下文；把句子打乱会破坏这两者。**数据是方法的一部分**——这个认识后来在 GPT-3、LLaMA 的数据工程中被放大到极致。
SUB@@3.2 Fine-tuning BERT@@3.2 微调 BERT
P@@Fine-tuning is straightforward since the self-attention mechanism in the Transformer allows BERT to model many downstream tasks—whether they involve single text or text pairs—by swapping out the appropriate inputs and outputs. For applications involving text pairs, a common pattern is to independently encode text pairs before applying bidirectional cross attention, such as Parikh et al. (2016); Seo et al. (2017). BERT instead uses the self-attention mechanism to unify these two stages, as encoding a concatenated text pair with self-attention effectively includes bidirectional cross attention between two sentences.@@微调非常直接：Transformer 的自注意力机制使 BERT 只需替换相应的输入与输出，就能建模许多下游任务——无论涉及单段文本还是文本对。对于涉及文本对的应用，常见做法是先<strong>独立编码</strong>两段文本，再施加双向交叉注意力（如 Parikh 等，2016；Seo 等，2017）。BERT 则用自注意力机制<strong>把这两个阶段统一起来</strong>——把拼接后的文本对用自注意力编码，实际上就已经包含了两句话之间的双向交叉注意力。
P@@For each task, we simply plug in the task-specific inputs and outputs into BERT and fine-tune all the parameters end-to-end. At the input, sentence A and sentence B from pre-training are analogous to (1) sentence pairs in paraphrasing, (2) hypothesis-premise pairs in entailment, (3) question-passage pairs in question answering, and (4) a degenerate text-∅ pair in text classification or sequence tagging. At the output, the token representations are fed into an output layer for token-level tasks, such as sequence tagging or question answering, and the [CLS] representation is fed into an output layer for classification, such as entailment or sentiment analysis.@@对每个任务，我们只需把任务专用的输入与输出接入 BERT，然后端到端微调所有参数。在输入端，预训练时的句子 A 与 B 分别对应：(1) 复述任务中的句子对；(2) 蕴含任务中的假设—前提对；(3) 问答中的问句—段落对；(4) 文本分类或序列标注中退化的「文本—空」对。在输出端，词元表示被送入输出层以完成词元级任务（如序列标注或问答），而 [CLS] 表示被送入输出层以完成分类任务（如蕴含判断或情感分析）。
P@@Compared to pre-training, fine-tuning is relatively inexpensive. All of the results in the paper can be replicated in at most 1 hour on a single Cloud TPU, or a few hours on a GPU, starting from the exact same pre-trained model. We describe the task-specific details in the corresponding subsections of Section 4. More details can be found in Appendix A.5.@@与预训练相比，微调成本相对低廉。从完全相同的预训练模型出发，本文所有结果都能在单块 Cloud TPU 上最多 1 小时、或在 GPU 上几小时内复现。我们在第 4 节的相应小节描述各任务细节，更多细节见附录 A.5。
NOTE@@<b>预训练 4 天（16 块 TPU），微调 1 小时</b>——这个不对称性是整个范式的经济学基础：昂贵的知识压缩做一次，便宜的适配做很多次。今天「预训练一个大模型，无数人做 LoRA 微调」完全是同一套逻辑。

SUB@@A.2 Pre-training Procedure@@A.2 预训练流程
P@@To generate each training input sequence, we sample two spans of text from the corpus, which we refer to as "sentences" even though they are typically much longer than single sentences (but can be shorter also). The first sentence receives the A embedding and the second receives the B embedding. 50% of the time B is the actual next sentence that follows A and 50% of the time it is a random sentence, which is done for the "next sentence prediction" task. They are sampled such that the combined length is ≤512 tokens. The LM masking is applied after WordPiece tokenization with a uniform masking rate of 15%, and no special consideration given to partial word pieces.@@为生成每个训练输入序列，我们从语料库中采样两段文本，称之为「句子」——尽管它们通常比单句长得多（也可能更短）。第一句使用 A 嵌入，第二句使用 B 嵌入。50% 的情况下 B 是紧跟 A 的真实下一句，50% 的情况下是随机句子，用于「下一句预测」任务。采样时保证两者总长度 ≤ 512 个词元。LM 遮盖在 WordPiece 分词之后进行，遮盖率统一为 15%，对不完整子词不做特殊处理。
P@@We train with batch size of 256 sequences (256 sequences * 512 tokens = 128,000 tokens/batch) for 1,000,000 steps, which is approximately 40 epochs over the 3.3 billion word corpus. We use Adam with learning rate of 1e-4, β<sub>1</sub> = 0.9, β<sub>2</sub> = 0.999, L2 weight decay of 0.01, learning rate warmup over the first 10,000 steps, and linear decay of the learning rate. We use a dropout probability of 0.1 on all layers. We use a gelu activation (Hendrycks and Gimpel, 2016) rather than the standard relu, following OpenAI GPT. The training loss is the sum of the mean masked LM likelihood and the mean next sentence prediction likelihood.@@训练使用批大小 256 个序列（256 × 512 = 128,000 词元/批），共 1,000,000 步，约相当于在 33 亿词语料上训练 40 个 epoch。优化器为 Adam：学习率 1e-4，β<sub>1</sub>=0.9，β<sub>2</sub>=0.999，L2 权重衰减 0.01，前 10,000 步学习率预热，之后线性衰减。所有层 dropout 概率 0.1。沿用 OpenAI GPT，使用 gelu 激活函数（Hendrycks 和 Gimpel，2016）而非标准 relu。训练损失为掩码语言模型平均似然与下一句预测平均似然之和。
P@@Training of BERT<sub>BASE</sub> was performed on 4 Cloud TPUs in Pod configuration (16 TPU chips total). Training of BERT<sub>LARGE</sub> was performed on 16 Cloud TPUs (64 TPU chips total). Each pre-training took 4 days to complete. Longer sequences are disproportionately expensive because attention is quadratic to the sequence length. To speed up pretraining in our experiments, we pre-train the model with sequence length of 128 for 90% of the steps. Then, we train the rest 10% of the steps of sequence of 512 to learn the positional embeddings.@@BERT<sub>BASE</sub> 在 4 块 Cloud TPU（Pod 配置，共 16 个 TPU 芯片）上训练；BERT<sub>LARGE</sub> 在 16 块 Cloud TPU（共 64 个芯片）上训练。每次预训练耗时 4 天。更长序列的代价不成比例地高，因为注意力复杂度与序列长度平方成正比。为加速实验中的预训练，我们 90% 的步数使用序列长度 128，剩余 10% 的步数使用长度 512，以学习位置嵌入。
NOTE@@<b>「90% 用 128、10% 用 512」是一个很实用的工程 trick</b>：既省了 4 倍算力，又让模型见过长序列的位置嵌入。今天训练长上下文模型时的课程学习（curriculum）、混合长度批次，都能看到这种思路的影子。

P@@For fine-tuning, most model hyperparameters are the same as in pre-training, with the exception of the batch size, learning rate, and number of training epochs.
