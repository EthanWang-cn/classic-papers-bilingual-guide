# ============ 1 Introduction ============
CH@@1@@1. Introduction@@1. 引言
LEAD@@引言在做一件很重要的事：<strong>论证"为什么是现在"</strong>。三个条件同时成熟——数据（ImageNet 百万级标注）、算力（GPU）、模型（CNN 的先验假设）——缺一不可。这段也是理解整个深度学习时代的钥匙。
P@@Current approaches to object recognition make essential use of machine learning methods. To improve their performance, we can collect larger datasets, learn more powerful models, and use better techniques for preventing overfitting. Until recently, datasets of labeled images were relatively small — on the order of tens of thousands of images (e.g., NORB, Caltech-101/256, and CIFAR-10/100). Simple recognition tasks can be solved quite well with datasets of this size, especially if they are augmented with label-preserving transformations.@@当前的目标识别方法从根本上依赖机器学习。要提升性能，我们可以收集更大的数据集、学习更强大的模型，以及采用更好的防止过拟合的技术。直到最近，带标注的图像数据集还相对较小——大约几万张量级（例如 NORB、Caltech-101/256 和 CIFAR-10/100）。这种规模的数据集足以很好地解决简单的识别任务，尤其是当用保标签的变换做数据增强时。
P@@For example, the current-best error rate on the MNIST digit-recognition task (<0.3%) approaches human performance. But objects in realistic settings exhibit considerable variability, so to learn to recognize them it is necessary to use much larger training sets. And indeed, the shortcomings of small image datasets have been widely recognized, but it has only recently become possible to collect labeled datasets with millions of images.@@例如，MNIST 手写数字识别任务当前的最佳错误率（&lt;0.3%）已接近人类水平。但真实场景中的物体具有相当大的变异性，因此要学会识别它们，必须使用大得多的训练集。事实上，小图像数据集的缺陷早已被广泛认识，但直到最近才有可能收集到数百万张图像的标注数据集。
P@@The new larger datasets include LabelMe, which consists of hundreds of thousands of fully-segmented images, and ImageNet, which consists of over 15 million labeled high-resolution images in over 22,000 categories.@@新的大型数据集包括 LabelMe（含数十万张完全分割的图像）和 ImageNet（含超过 22000 个类别、1500 万张带标注的高分辨率图像）。
NOTE@@注意数字对比：此前主流数据集是"几万张"，ImageNet 是"1500 万张"——<b>三个数量级的跃迁</b>。这是全文的隐藏主角：没有这场数据爆炸，再好的算法也无处施展。
P@@To learn about thousands of objects from millions of images, we need a model with a large learning capacity. However, the immense complexity of the object recognition task means that this problem cannot be specified even by a dataset as large as ImageNet, so our model should also have lots of prior knowledge to compensate for all the data we don't have.@@要从数百万张图像中学习数千种物体，我们需要一个具有大学习容量的模型。然而，目标识别任务极其复杂，这意味着即便有 ImageNet 这样大的数据集也无法完全刻画这个问题，因此我们的模型还需要大量先验知识，以弥补我们缺失的那部分数据。
P@@Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) constitute one such class of models. Their capacity can be controlled by varying their depth and breadth, and they also make strong and mostly correct assumptions about the nature of images (namely, stationarity of statistics and locality of pixel dependencies). Thus, compared to standard feedforward neural networks with similarly-sized layers, CNNs have much fewer connections and parameters and so they are easier to train, while their theoretically-best performance is likely to be only slightly worse.@@卷积神经网络（CNN）就是这样一类模型。它们的容量可以通过改变深度和宽度来控制，并且对图像的性质做出了强且基本正确的假设（即统计量的平稳性与像素依赖的局部性）。因此，与层规模相当的标准前馈神经网络相比，CNN 的连接和参数要少得多，因而更容易训练，而其理论最优性能可能只是略逊一筹。
NOTE@@<b>这里是全文最重要的理论句</b>：CNN 的"归纳偏置"（inductive bias）——局部性 + 权值共享 + 平移不变性——就是你"没有的数据"。全连接网络要把"A 出现在左上角"和"A 出现在右下角"当两件事从头学，CNN 从架构上就假定它们是同一件事。今天理解 CNN 为何样本效率高，仍要回到这句话。
P@@Despite the attractive qualities of CNNs, and despite the relative efficiency of their local architecture, they have still been prohibitively expensive to apply in large scale to high-resolution images. Luckily, current GPUs, paired with a highly-optimized implementation of 2D convolution, are powerful enough to facilitate the training of interestingly-large CNNs, and recent datasets such as ImageNet contain enough labeled examples to train such models without severe overfitting.@@尽管 CNN 有这些吸引人的品质，尽管其局部架构相对高效，但把它大规模应用于高分辨率图像仍然昂贵到难以承受。幸运的是，当前的 GPU 配上高度优化的二维卷积实现，已足够强大，能够训练规模可观的 CNN；而像 ImageNet 这样的新近数据集也包含足够的标注样本，可以训练这类模型而不至于严重过拟合。
P@@The specific contributions of this paper are as follows: we trained one of the largest convolutional neural networks to date on the subsets of ImageNet used in the ILSVRC-2010 and ILSVRC-2012 competitions and achieved by far the best results ever reported on these datasets. We wrote a highly-optimized GPU implementation of 2D convolution and all the other operations inherent in training convolutional neural networks, which we make available publicly.@@本文的具体贡献如下：我们在 ILSVRC-2010 和 ILSVRC-2012 竞赛所用的 ImageNet 子集上，训练了迄今为止最大的卷积神经网络之一，并在这些数据集上取得了远优于此前所有报告的结果。我们编写了高度优化的二维卷积及训练 CNN 所需的其他所有操作的 GPU 实现，并已公开。
P@@Our network contains a number of new and unusual features which improve its performance and reduce its training time, which are detailed in Section 3. The size of our network made overfitting a significant problem, even with 1.2 million labeled training examples, so we used several effective techniques for preventing overfitting, which are described in Section 4. Our final network contains five convolutional and three fully-connected layers, and this depth seems to be important: we found that removing any convolutional layer (each of which contains no more than 1% of the model's parameters) resulted in inferior performance.@@我们的网络包含若干新颖且不寻常的特性，可提升性能并缩短训练时间，详见第 3 节。即便有 120 万个标注训练样本，网络规模仍使过拟合成为重大问题，因此我们采用了若干有效的防过拟合技术，详见第 4 节。最终网络包含五个卷积层和三个全连接层，这个深度似乎很重要：我们发现移除任何一个卷积层（每层参数不超过模型参数的 1%）都会导致性能下降。
P@@In the end, the network's size is limited mainly by the amount of memory available on current GPUs and by the amount of training time that we are willing to tolerate. Our network takes between five and six days to train on two GTX 580 3GB GPUs. All of our experiments suggest that our results can be improved simply by waiting for faster GPUs and bigger datasets to become available.@@最终，网络规模主要受限于当前 GPU 的显存容量和我们愿意容忍的训练时长。我们的网络在两块 GTX 580 3GB GPU 上需要训练五到六天。所有实验都表明，只要等待更快的 GPU 和更大的数据集出现，我们的结果就能进一步提升。
NOTE@@最后一句是全文的"时代宣言"：<b>把进步寄托在算力与数据的增长上</b>。2012 年这是大胆预判，此后十年被完全印证——从 AlexNet 到 GPT，主线一直是"更大模型 + 更多数据 + 更强算力"。这句话也是理解 Scaling Law 的思想源头。
ENDCH

CH@@2@@2. The Dataset@@2. 数据集
LEAD@@这一节是"实验规范"：ILSVRC 用 1000 类、每类约 1000 张，共 120 万训练图。注意作者解释为什么主要用 2010 版——因为只有它有公开的测试标签。这种对评测细节的较真，本身就是好论文的标志。
P@@ImageNet is a dataset of over 15 million labeled high-resolution images belonging to roughly 22,000 categories. The images were collected from the web and labeled by human labelers using Amazon's Mechanical Turk crowd-sourcing tool. Starting in 2010, as part of the Pascal Visual Object Challenge, an annual competition called the ImageNet Large-Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) has been held.@@ImageNet 是一个包含超过 1500 万张带标注高分辨率图像、约 22000 个类别的数据集。图像从网络收集，由人工标注者通过 Amazon Mechanical Turk 众包工具完成标注。自 2010 年起，作为 Pascal Visual Object Challenge 的一部分，每年举办一次名为 ImageNet 大规模视觉识别挑战赛（ILSVRC）的竞赛。
P@@ILSVRC uses a subset of ImageNet with roughly 1000 images in each of 1000 categories. In all, there are roughly 1.2 million training images, 50,000 validation images, and 150,000 testing images.@@ILSVRC 使用 ImageNet 的一个子集：1000 个类别，每类约 1000 张图。总计约 120 万张训练图像、5 万张验证图像和 15 万张测试图像。
P@@ILSVRC-2010 is the only version of ILSVRC for which the test set labels are available, so this is the version on which we performed most of our experiments. Since we also entered our model in the ILSVRC-2012 competition, in Section 6 we report our results on this version of the dataset as well, for which test set labels are unavailable.@@ILSVRC-2010 是唯一一个测试集标签公开的版本，因此我们用它完成了大部分实验。由于我们也参加了 ILSVRC-2012 竞赛，第 6 节同样报告了在该版本数据集上的结果（其测试集标签不公开）。
P@@On ImageNet, it is customary to report two error rates: top-1 and top-5, where the top-5 error rate is the fraction of test images for which the correct label is not among the five labels considered most probable by the model.@@在 ImageNet 上，习惯报告两种错误率：top-1 和 top-5。其中 top-5 错误率是指这样一类测试图像的比例：正确标签不在模型认为最可能的五个标签之中。
P@@ImageNet consists of variable-resolution images, while our system requires a constant input dimensionality. Therefore, we down-sampled the images to a fixed resolution of 256 × 256. Given a rectangular image, we first rescaled the image such that the shorter side was of length 256, and then cropped out the central 256×256 patch from the resulting image. We did not pre-process the images in any other way, except for subtracting the mean activity over the training set from each pixel. So we trained our network on the (centered) raw RGB values of the pixels.@@ImageNet 由分辨率不一的图像组成，而我们的系统要求输入维度恒定。因此我们把图像下采样到固定的 256×256 分辨率。对于矩形图像，先缩放使较短边长度为 256，再从结果图像中裁出中央的 256×256 区域。除对每个像素减去训练集上的平均激活值外，我们没有做任何其它预处理。也就是说，我们直接在（中心化后的）原始 RGB 像素值上训练网络。
NOTE@@"没有做其它预处理"是刻意强调的：AlexNet 之前，视觉流水线充满手工设计的特征（SIFT、Fisher Vector）。这里的宣言是<b>端到端学习</b>——从原始像素直接到类别，中间特征由网络自己学。理解这一点，才能理解为什么这篇是分水岭。

SUB@@3.1 ReLU Nonlinearity@@3.1 ReLU 非线性
P@@The standard way to model a neuron's output f as a function of its input x is with f(x) = tanh(x) or f(x) = (1 + e^(−x))^(−1). In terms of training time with gradient descent, these saturating nonlinearities are much slower than the non-saturating nonlinearity f(x) = max(0, x). Following Nair and Hinton, we refer to neurons with this nonlinearity as Rectified Linear Units (ReLUs).@@把神经元输出 f 建模为输入 x 的函数的标准做法，是用 f(x) = tanh(x) 或 f(x) = (1 + e^(−x))^(−1)。就梯度下降的训练时间而言，这些<strong>饱和</strong>非线性比非饱和非线性 f(x) = max(0, x) 慢得多。沿用 Nair 和 Hinton 的叫法，我们把采用这种非线性的神经元称为<strong>修正线性单元（ReLU）</strong>。
P@@Deep convolutional neural networks with ReLUs train several times faster than their equivalents with tanh units. This is demonstrated in Figure 1, which shows the number of iterations required to reach 25% training error on the CIFAR-10 dataset for a particular four-layer convolutional network. This plot shows that we would not have been able to experiment with such large neural networks for this work if we had used traditional saturating neuron models.@@采用 ReLU 的深度卷积网络比使用 tanh 单元的等价网络训练快数倍。图 1 展示了这一点：对一个特定的四层卷积网络，在 CIFAR-10 数据集上达到 25% 训练误差所需的迭代次数。该图表明，如果当时使用传统的饱和神经元模型，我们根本不可能在这个工作中试验如此大的神经网络。
FIG@@p3_fig1.png@@Figure 1. A four-layer CNN with ReLUs (solid) reaches 25% training error on CIFAR-10 six times faster than an equivalent network with tanh neurons (dashed).@@图 1 · 用 ReLU 的四层 CNN（实线）在 CIFAR-10 上达到 25% 训练误差的速度，比用 tanh 神经元的等价网络（虚线）快六倍。学习率各自独立调优以使训练尽可能快，未使用任何正则化。@@注意"六倍"这个数字：它不是最终结果更好，而是<b>同样结果来得更快</b>。在算力昂贵的年代，快六倍意味着原本一周的实验变成一天——这直接决定了"能不能试这么大的模型"。
P@@We are not the first to consider alternatives to traditional neuron models in CNNs. For example, Jarrett et al. claim that the nonlinearity f(x) = |tanh(x)| works particularly well with their type of contrast normalization followed by local average pooling on the Caltech-101 dataset. However, on this dataset the primary concern is preventing overfitting, so the effect they are observing is different from the accelerated ability to fit the training set which we report when using ReLUs. Faster learning has a great influence on the performance of large models trained on large datasets.@@我们并非最早考虑在 CNN 中替代传统神经元模型的人。例如 Jarrett 等人声称，在 Caltech-101 数据集上，非线性 f(x) = |tanh(x)| 配合他们那种对比度归一化再接局部平均池化，效果特别好。然而在那个数据集上，主要关注的是防止过拟合，因此他们观察到的效果，与我们报告的使用 ReLU 带来的"加速拟合训练集"的能力是不同的。更快的学习对在大数据集上训练的大模型性能有巨大影响。
NOTE@@<b>理解要点</b>：为什么 tanh 慢？tanh 在两端饱和区梯度接近 0，信号传不回去；ReLU 在 x&gt;0 时梯度恒为 1，一条"梯度高速路"直通底层。这一简单改动是今天所有深度学习的基础默认激活函数，其价值在 2012 年被这篇论文用大规模实验坐实。
SUB@@3.2 Training on Multiple GPUs@@3.2 在多 GPU 上训练
P@@A single GTX 580 GPU has only 3GB of memory, which limits the maximum size of the networks that can be trained on it. It turns out that 1.2 million training examples are enough to train networks which are too big to fit on one GPU. Therefore we spread the net across two GPUs. Current GPUs are particularly well-suited to cross-GPU parallelization, as they are able to read from and write to one another's memory directly, without going through host machine memory.@@单块 GTX 580 GPU 只有 3GB 显存，限制了可在其上训练的网络的最大规模。事实证明，120 万个训练样本足以训练大到无法装进单块 GPU 的网络。因此我们把网络拆分到两块 GPU 上。当前的 GPU 特别适合跨 GPU 并行，因为它们能够直接读写彼此的显存，而无需经过主机内存。
P@@The parallelization scheme that we employ essentially puts half of the kernels (or neurons) on each GPU, with one additional trick: the GPUs communicate only in certain layers. This means that, for example, the kernels of layer 3 take input from all kernel maps in layer 2. However, kernels in layer 4 take input only from those kernel maps in layer 3 which reside on the same GPU. Choosing the pattern of connectivity is a problem for cross-validation, but this allows us to precisely tune the amount of communication until it is an acceptable fraction of the amount of computation.@@我们采用的并行方案本质上是在每块 GPU 上放置一半的卷积核（或神经元），另加一个小技巧：GPU 只在特定层通信。这意味着，例如第 3 层的卷积核接收第 2 层所有卷积核映射的输入；而第 4 层的卷积核只接收同一 GPU 上第 3 层的那些卷积核映射的输入。选择何种连接模式本是个需要交叉验证的问题，但这让我们能精确调节通信量，使其在计算量中占可接受的比例。
P@@The resultant architecture is somewhat similar to that of the "columnar" CNN employed by Ciresan et al., except that our columns are not independent. This scheme reduces our top-1 and top-5 error rates by 1.7% and 1.2%, respectively, as compared with a net with half as many kernels in each convolutional layer trained on one GPU. The two-GPU net takes slightly less time to train than the one-GPU net.@@由此得到的架构与 Ciresan 等人采用的"柱状"CNN 有些相似，区别在于我们的"柱"并非相互独立。与在单 GPU 上训练、每个卷积层卷积核数量减半的网络相比，该方案将 top-1 和 top-5 错误率分别降低了 1.7% 和 1.2%。双 GPU 网络的训练时间还略短于单 GPU 网络。
NOTE@@这段今天读来像"考古"：2012 年 3GB 显存是硬约束，作者被迫把网络劈成两半。但它揭示了一个永恒权衡——<b>通信 vs 计算</b>。今天的 tensord/model parallelism 依然是同一个问题的更大规模版本。顺带一提，两个 GPU 学到的卷积核出现了分工：GPU1 学颜色无关的边缘，GPU2 学颜色特异的斑块（见 6.1 节）。

P@@The ordering of the kernel maps is of course arbitrary and determined before training begins. The constants k, n, α, and β are hyper-parameters whose values are determined using a validation set; we used k = 2, n = 5, α = 10^(−4), and β = 0.75. P@@This scheme bears some resemblance to the local contrast normalization scheme of Jarrett et al., but ours would be more correctly termed "brightness normalization", since we do not subtract the mean activity. NOTE@@<b>历史视角</b>：LRN 是今天几乎没人用的组件（BatchNorm 2015 年取代了它）。但它属于那类"结论正确、具体实现过时"的工作——这篇论文最有价值的不是教你用 LRN，而是展示了<b>系统性消融实验</b>的做法：每个组件单独报告提升多少。
SUB@@3.4 Overlapping Pooling@@3.4 重叠池化
P@@Pooling layers in CNNs summarize the outputs of neighboring groups of neurons in the same kernel map. Traditionally, the neighborhoods summarized by adjacent pooling units do not overlap. To be more precise, a pooling layer can be thought of as consisting of a grid of pooling units spaced s pixels apart, each summarizing a neighborhood of size z × z centered at the location of the pooling unit. If we set s = z, we obtain traditional local pooling as commonly employed in CNNs. If we set s < z, we obtain overlapping pooling.@@CNN 中的池化层汇总同一卷积核映射内相邻神经元组的输出。传统上，相邻池化单元汇总的邻域互不重叠。更准确地说，池化层可视为由间隔 s 像素排列的池化单元网格构成，每个单元汇总以自身位置为中心、大小为 z×z 的邻域。若令 s = z，就得到 CNN 中常用的传统局部池化；若令 s &lt; z，就得到<strong>重叠池化</strong>。
P@@This is what we use throughout the network, with s = 2 and z = 3. This scheme reduces the top-1 and top-5 error rates by 0.4% and 0.3%, respectively, as compared with the non-overlapping scheme s = 2, z = 2, which produces output of equivalent dimensions. We generally observe during training that models with overlapping pooling find it slightly more difficult to overfit.@@我们在整个网络中使用后者，取 s=2、z=3。与产生同等维度输出的非重叠方案 s=2、z=2 相比，该方案将 top-1 和 top-5 错误率分别降低了 0.4% 和 0.3%。我们在训练中普遍观察到，采用重叠池化的模型稍微更难过拟合。
SUB@@3.5 Overall Architecture@@3.5 整体架构
P@@Now we are ready to describe the overall architecture of our CNN. As depicted in Figure 2, the net contains eight layers with weights; the first five are convolutional and the remaining three are fully-connected. The output of the last fully-connected layer is fed to a 1000-way softmax which produces a distribution over the 1000 class labels. Our network maximizes the multinomial logistic regression objective, which is equivalent to maximizing the average across training cases of the log-probability of the correct label under the prediction distribution.@@现在可以描述 CNN 的整体架构了。如图 2 所示，网络包含八带权重的层：前五层是卷积层，其余三层是全连接层。最后一个全连接层的输出送入 1000 路 softmax，产生 1000 个类别标签上的分布。我们的网络最大化多项逻辑回归目标，这等价于在所有训练样本上，最大化正确标签在预测分布下的平均对数概率。
P@@The kernels of the second, fourth, and fifth convolutional layers are connected only to those kernel maps in the previous layer which reside on the same GPU. The kernels of the third convolutional layer are connected to all kernel maps in the second layer. The neurons in the fully-connected layers are connected to all neurons in the previous layer. Response-normalization layers follow the first and second convolutional layers. Max-pooling layers follow both response-normalization layers as well as the fifth convolutional layer. The ReLU non-linearity is applied to the output of every convolutional and fully-connected layer.@@第二、第四和第五卷积层的卷积核只连接到同一 GPU 上前一层的卷积核映射；第三卷积层的卷积核连接到第二层的所有卷积核映射；全连接层的神经元连接到前一层的所有神经元。响应归一化层紧跟第一、第二卷积层之后；最大池化层紧跟这两个归一化层以及第五卷积层之后；每个卷积层和全连接层的输出都施加 ReLU 非线性。
P@@The first convolutional layer filters the 224×224×3 input image with 96 kernels of size 11×11×3 with a stride of 4 pixels. The second convolutional layer takes as input the (response-normalized and pooled) output of the first convolutional layer and filters it with 256 kernels of size 5 × 5 × 48. The third, fourth, and fifth convolutional layers are connected to one another without any intervening pooling or normalization layers. The third convolutional layer has 384 kernels of size 3 × 3 × 256 connected to the (normalized, pooled) outputs of the second convolutional layer. The fourth convolutional layer has 384 kernels of size 3 × 3 × 192, and the fifth convolutional layer has 256 kernels of size 3 × 3 × 192. The fully-connected layers have 4096 neurons each.@@第一个卷积层用 96 个大小为 11×11×3、步长为 4 像素的卷积核对 224×224×3 的输入图像做滤波。第二个卷积层以第一层的输出（经响应归一化与池化）为输入，用 256 个 5×5×48 的卷积核滤波。第三、第四、第五卷积层彼此直接相连，中间没有任何池化或归一化层。第三卷积层有 384 个 3×3×256 的卷积核，连接第二层的（归一化、池化后）输出；第四卷积层有 384 个 3×3×192 的卷积核；第五卷积层有 256 个 3×3×192 的卷积核。全连接层各有 4096 个神经元。

# ============ 4 Reducing Overfitting ============
CH@@4@@4. Reducing Overfitting@@4. 减少过拟合
LEAD@@6000 万参数 vs 120 万样本，过拟合是生死问题。作者给出两把武器：<strong>数据增强</strong>（几乎免费）和 <strong>Dropout</strong>（约两倍训练时间）。这一节是今天每个 CV 工程师仍在用的实践。
P@@Our neural network architecture has 60 million parameters. Although the 1000 classes of ILSVRC make each training example impose 10 bits of constraint on the mapping from image to label, this turns out to be insufficient to learn so many parameters without considerable overfitting. Below, we describe the two primary ways in which we combat overfitting.@@我们的网络架构有 6000 万个参数。尽管 ILSVRC 的 1000 个类别使每个训练样本对"图像到标签"的映射施加约 10 比特的约束，但事实证明，这仍不足以在学习如此多参数的同时避免严重过拟合。下面描述我们对抗过拟合的两种主要方法。
SUB@@4.1 Data Augmentation@@4.1 数据增强
P@@The easiest and most common method to reduce overfitting on image data is to artificially enlarge the dataset using label-preserving transformations. We employ two distinct forms of data augmentation, both of which allow transformed images to be produced from the original images with very little computation, so the transformed images do not need to be stored on disk. In our implementation, the transformed images are generated in Python code on the CPU while the GPU is training on the previous batch of images. So these data augmentation schemes are, in effect, computationally free.@@减少图像数据过拟合最简单、最常用的方法，是用保标签变换人为扩大数据集。我们采用两种不同形式的数据增强，二者都能以极低计算量从原图生成变换后的图像，因此无需把变换图像存到磁盘上。在我们的实现中，变换图像由 CPU 上的 Python 代码生成，与此同时 GPU 正在训练上一批图像。因此这些数据增强方案实际上在计算上是"免费"的。
P@@The first form of data augmentation consists of generating image translations and horizontal reflections. We do this by extracting random 224 × 224 patches (and their horizontal reflections) from the 256×256 images and training our network on these extracted patches. This increases the size of our training set by a factor of 2048, though the resulting training examples are, of course, highly interdependent. Without this scheme, our network suffers from substantial overfitting, which would have forced us to use much smaller networks. At test time, the network makes a prediction by extracting five 224 × 224 patches (the four corner patches and the center patch) as well as their horizontal reflections (hence ten patches in all), and averaging the predictions made by the network's softmax layer on the ten patches.@@第一种数据增强是生成图像平移和水平翻转。我们从 256×256 图像中随机裁出 224×224 的图块（及其水平翻转），并在这些图块上训练网络。这使训练集规模扩大了 2048 倍，当然，生成的训练样本之间高度相关。没有这个方案，网络会严重过拟合，迫使我们使用小得多的网络。测试时，网络通过裁出五个 224×224 图块（四角加中心）及它们的水平翻转（共十个图块），并对十个图块在 softmax 层的预测取平均来做出预测。
NOTE@@<b>2048 倍怎么来的？</b>从 256×256 里裁 224×224，水平和垂直各有 256−224+1 = 33 个位置，33×33 = 1089，再乘水平翻转 2 ≈ 2178，作者近似写 2048（约 2¹¹）。这种"用裁剪 + 翻转换取样本量"的做法，今天仍是训练 CNN 的标准起手式。测试时的"十裁剪平均"也是当年的标配 TTA（测试时增强）。
P@@The second form of data augmentation consists of altering the intensities of the RGB channels in training images. Specifically, we perform PCA on the set of RGB pixel values throughout the ImageNet training set. To each training image, we add multiples of the found principal components, with magnitudes proportional to the corresponding eigenvalues times a random variable drawn from a Gaussian with mean zero and standard deviation 0.1.@@第二种数据增强是改变训练图像中 RGB 通道的强度。具体来说，我们在整个 ImageNet 训练集的 RGB 像素值集合上做主成分分析（PCA）。对每张训练图像，我们加入找到的主成分的若干倍，其幅度与对应特征值成正比，再乘以一个从均值 0、标准差 0.1 的高斯分布中抽取的随机变量。
EQ@@I<sub>xy</sub> = [I<sup>R</sup><sub>xy</sub>, I<sup>G</sup><sub>xy</sub>, I<sup>B</sup><sub>xy</sub>]<sup>T</sup> &nbsp;→&nbsp; I<sub>xy</sub> + [p<sub>1</sub>, p<sub>2</sub>, p<sub>3</sub>] [α<sub>1</sub>λ<sub>1</sub>, α<sub>2</sub>λ<sub>2</sub>, α<sub>3</sub>λ<sub>3</sub>]<sup>T</sup>@@@@pᵢ 和 λᵢ 分别是 RGB 像素值 3×3 协方差矩阵的第 i 个特征向量和特征值，αᵢ 是上述随机变量。每张图像只在首次使用时抽一次 α（对全图所有像素共用），再次用到时重抽。它近似捕捉了自然图像的一条重要性质：物体身份对光照强度和颜色变化是不变的。这一方案使 top-1 错误率降低超过 1%。
P@@Each αᵢ is drawn only once for all the pixels of a particular training image until that image is used for training again, at which point it is re-drawn. This scheme approximately captures an important property of natural images, namely, that object identity is invariant to changes in the intensity and color of the illumination. This scheme reduces the top-1 error rate by over 1%.@@每个 αᵢ 对某张特定训练图像的所有像素只抽取一次，直到该图像再次被用于训练时才重新抽取。该方案近似捕捉了自然图像的一个重要性质：物体身份对光照强度与颜色的变化具有不变性。这一方案使 top-1 错误率降低了 1% 以上。
NOTE@@这个"PCA 颜色扰动"今天很少单独拎出来讲，但它是<b>颜色增强</b>的早期形式。想法很聪明：不要随机乱改 RGB，而是沿自然图像颜色协方差的主方向扰动——这样生成的变化更符合真实光照变化。今天 AutoAugment/ColorJitter 是它的后继者。
SUB@@4.2 Dropout@@4.2 Dropout
P@@Combining the predictions of many different models is a very successful way to reduce test errors, but it appears to be too expensive for big neural networks that already take several days to train. There is, however, a very efficient version of model combination that only costs about a factor of two during training. The recently-introduced technique, called "dropout", consists of setting to zero the output of each hidden neuron with probability 0.5.@@组合许多不同模型的预测是降低测试误差非常成功的方法，但对已经需要数天才能训练的大神经网络来说，这似乎太昂贵了。然而，有一种非常高效的模型组合版本，其训练成本仅约为两倍。这项新近提出的技術称为"dropout"：以 0.5 的概率把每个隐藏神经元的输出置零。
P@@The neurons which are "dropped out" in this way do not contribute to the forward pass and do not participate in back-propagation. So every time an input is presented, the neural network samples a different architecture, but all these architectures share weights. This technique reduces complex co-adaptations of neurons, since a neuron cannot rely on the presence of particular other neurons. It is, therefore, forced to learn more robust features that are useful in conjunction with many different random subsets of the other neurons.@@以这种方式被"丢弃"的神经元不参与前向传播，也不参与反向传播。因此每次输入呈现时，神经网络都采样出一个不同的架构，但所有这些架构共享权重。该技术减少了神经元之间复杂的"共适应"（co-adaptation），因为一个神经元不能再依赖某些特定其它神经元的存在。于是它被强迫学习更鲁棒的特征——这些特征要能与其它神经元的许多不同随机子集协同工作。
P@@At test time, we use all the neurons but multiply their outputs by 0.5, which is a reasonable approximation to taking the geometric mean of the predictive distributions produced by the exponentially-many dropout networks. We use dropout in the first two fully-connected layers of Figure 2. Without dropout, our network exhibits substantial overfitting. Dropout roughly doubles the number of iterations required to converge.@@测试时，我们使用所有神经元，但把它们的输出乘以 0.5，这是对指数级数量的 dropout 网络所产生的预测分布取几何平均的一个合理近似。我们在图 2 的前两个全连接层使用 dropout。没有 dropout，网络会表现出严重过拟合。dropout 大约使收敛所需的迭代次数翻倍。
NOTE@@<b>为什么 dropout 有效？</b> 两种理解都值得记住：① <b>集成视角</b>——每次前向都在采样一个子网络，指数多个子网络共享权重，测试时的 0.5 缩放近似它们的几何平均；② <b>共适应视角</b>——禁止神经元形成"依赖某个特定伙伴"的脆弱组合，逼它学到单独也有用的特征。第二个视角解释了为什么 dropout 主要加在全连接层（那里参数密集、最容易共适应）。
ENDCH

# ============ 5 Details of learning ============
CH@@5@@5. Details of Learning@@5. 训练细节
LEAD@@这节是"炼丹配方"：SGD + 动量 0.9 + 权重衰减 0.0005 + 批次 128 + 手动学习率衰减。今天看很朴素，但注意一个洞见——作者发现权重衰减<strong>不只是正则项，还降低了训练误差</strong>。这种对实验细节的诚实观察，是这篇论文可信度的重要来源。
P@@We trained our models using stochastic gradient descent with a batch size of 128 examples, momentum of 0.9, and weight decay of 0.0005. SUB@@6.1 Qualitative Evaluations@@6.1 定性评估
P@@Figure 3 shows the convolutional kernels learned by the network's two data-connected layers. The network has learned a variety of frequency- and orientation-selective kernels, as well as various colored blobs. Notice the specialization exhibited by the two GPUs, a result of the restricted connectivity described in Section 3.5. The kernels on GPU 1 are largely color-agnostic, while the kernels on GPU 2 are largely color-specific. This kind of specialization occurs during every run and is independent of any particular random weight initialization.@@图 3 展示了网络两个与数据直接相连的层所学到的卷积核。网络学到了各种频率选择性和方向选择性的卷积核，以及各种彩色斑块。注意两块 GPU 表现出的专业化，这是 3.5 节所述受限连接的结果。GPU 1 上的卷积核大多与颜色无关，而 GPU 2 上的卷积核大多与颜色有关。这种专业化在每次运行中都会出现，与任何特定的随机权重初始化无关。
P@@In the left panel of Figure 4 we qualitatively assess what the network has learned by computing its top-5 predictions on eight test images. Notice that even off-center objects, such as the mite in the top-left, can be recognized by the net. Most of the top-5 labels appear reasonable. For example, only other types of cat are considered plausible labels for the leopard. In some cases (grille, cherry) there is genuine ambiguity about the intended focus of the photograph.@@在图 4 左图中，我们通过计算网络对八张测试图像的 top-5 预测，定性评估它学到了什么。请注意，即使是偏离画面中心的物体（如左上角的螨虫）也能被网络识别。大多数 top-5 标签看起来都合理。例如，对豹来说，只有其它种类的猫才被认为是可信的标签。在某些情况下（格栅、樱桃），照片的预期焦点确实存在歧义。

(Right) Five test images and the six nearest training images in last-hidden-layer feature space.@@图 4 · 左：八张测试图像及其五个最可能标签，每张下方为正确标签，其概率用红色柱表示。右：五张测试图像，以及在其最后隐藏层特征空间中距离最近的六张训练图像。@@右图是理解"深度特征"的经典证据：检索出的训练图像在像素上和查询图差得很远（狗和象姿态各异），但在 4096 维特征空间里很近。说明网络高层学到的不是"像素相似度"而是"语义相似度"。
P@@Another way to probe the network's visual knowledge is to consider the feature activations induced by an image at the last, 4096-dimensional hidden layer. If two images produce feature activation vectors with a small Euclidean separation, we can say that the higher levels of the neural network consider them to be similar. Figure 4 shows five images from the test set and the six images from the training set that are most similar to each of them according to this measure. Notice that at the pixel level, the retrieved training images are generally not close in L2 to the query images in the first column. For example, the retrieved dogs and elephants appear in a variety of poses.@@探究网络视觉知识的另一种方法，是考察图像在最后的 4096 维隐藏层上引发的特征激活。如果两张图像产生的特征激活向量欧氏距离很小，我们就可以说神经网络的高层认为它们相似。图 4 展示了测试集中的五张图像，以及按此度量与每张图像最相似的六张训练集图像。请注意，在像素层面上，检索出的训练图像通常与第一列的查询图像在 L2 距离上并不接近。例如，检索出的狗和大象呈现出各种姿态。
P@@Computing similarity by using Euclidean distance between two 4096-dimensional, real-valued vectors is inefficient, but it could be made efﬁcient by training an auto-encoder to compress these vectors to short binary codes. This should produce a much better image retrieval method than applying auto-encoders to the raw pixels, which does not make use of image labels and hence has a tendency to retrieve images with similar patterns of edges, whether or not they are semantically similar.@@用两个 4096 维实值向量之间的欧氏距离计算相似度效率不高，但可以通过训练一个自编码器把这些向量压缩成短的二进制编码来提升效率。这应该能产生比直接对原始像素用自编码器更好的图像检索方法——后者不利用图像标签，因而倾向于检索出边缘 pattern 相似的图像，而不管它们在语义上是否相似。

# ============ 7 Discussion ============
CH@@7@@7. Discussion@@7. 讨论
LEAD@@结论只有两段，但信息量很大：强调"深度真的很重要"、坦承没用无监督预训练、并把目光投向视频。十多年后回看，视频这条线至今仍是前沿。
P@@Our results show that a large, deep convolutional neural network is capable of achieving record-breaking results on a highly challenging dataset using purely supervised learning. It is notable that our network's performance degrades if a single convolutional layer is removed. For example, removing any of the middle layers results in a loss of about 2% for the top-1 performance of the network. So the depth really is important for achieving our results.@@我们的结果表明，一个大型深度卷积神经网络仅用纯监督学习，就能在极具挑战性的数据集上取得破纪录的结果。值得注意的是，如果移除任何一个卷积层，网络性能就会下降。例如，移除任何一个中间层都会使网络的 top-1 性能损失约 2%。因此，深度对于取得我们的结果确实很重要。
NOTE@@"移除一层掉 2%"这个消融很关键：它把"深"从经验之谈变成实验结论。三年后 ResNet 要解决的正是一个悖论——更深的网络反而更难训练、误差更高——而 ResNet 的答案是残差连接。把这两篇连起来读，脉络会非常清晰。
P@@To simplify our experiments, we did not use any unsupervised pre-training even though we expect that it will help, especially if we obtain enough computational power to significantly increase the size of the network without obtaining a corresponding increase in the amount of labeled data. Thus far, our results have improved as we have made our network larger and trained it longer but we still have many orders of magnitude to go in order to match the infero-temporal pathway of the human visual system. Ultimately we would like to use very large and deep convolutional nets on video sequences where the temporal structure provides very helpful information that is missing or far less obvious in static images.@@为简化实验，我们没有使用任何无监督预训练，尽管我们预计它会有所帮助——特别是当我们获得足够算力、能显著增大网络规模而标注数据没有相应增加时。到目前为止，随着网络变大、训练变长，我们的结果不断提升，但要匹配人类视觉系统的颞下通路，我们仍有好几个数量级的差距。最终，我们希望把非常大、非常深的卷积网络用于视频序列——在那里，静态图像中缺失或远不明显的时序结构能提供非常有用的信息。
NOTE@@<b>这篇论文的"未竟之事"后来都由别人完成了</b>：无监督/自监督预训练 → 2018 年的 BERT 与 MoCo/SimCLR；更大网络 + 更多数据 → Scaling Law；视频理解 → 至今仍是前沿。一篇经典论文最有价值的部分，往往是它诚实写下的"我们还没做到"。
